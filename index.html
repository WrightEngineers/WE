What we make

Parts that wear out first: the ones that carry load, turn, and rub.

Gears and sprockets

Spur gears and chain sprockets cut to your tooth specification, in the material your machine needs.

Shafts and splines

Stepped, keyed and splined shafts turned to your tolerances and finished to run true.

Bushes, sleeves and flanges

Plain bushes, sleeves and flanges made to size, in bronze, steel or stainless.
